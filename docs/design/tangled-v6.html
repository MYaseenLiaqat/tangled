Connect to your learning platform

Tangled runs inside the platform your students already use. Students ask questions there, and you see the results here.

Add the assistant to a course page

Paste this snippet into a course page or custom HTML block. The address below is an example.

<script src="https://cdn.tangled.example/widget.js"
  data-course="ai-ml-deep-learning"
  data-student-id="{{ anonymous_user_id }}"
  data-key="pk_live_xxxxxxxx" async></script>

How it works

1. EmbedYour platform shows the assistant next to the course materials.
2. Students askQuestions are answered from your materials and filed under a topic.
3. You reviewRepeated confusion appears on your Topics page. Students are never named.
