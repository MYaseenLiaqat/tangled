Why can a 1×3 NumPy array work with a 2×3 array?
NumPy broadcasting lets compatible shapes work together without manually copying the smaller array. A dimension of size 1 can be expanded conceptually to match the other array.
I still don't understand why the 1×3 array can be used for both rows. Can you explain it another way?
Think of it as one row of three values being applied across both rows of the 2×3 array. NumPy handles that alignment during the operation.
